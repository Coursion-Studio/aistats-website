AI Stats

Every AI limit — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot — at a glance. Nothing leaves your device.

Quick facts

  • What: Tracks usage, quotas, limits and spending across Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor and OpenRouter
  • Platform: macOS menu bar app and iOS app with Home/Lock Screen widgets; 27 languages
  • Privacy: Connects directly to each provider; nothing stored on any server — the developer cannot see your usage
  • Price: One-time purchase, no subscription
  • Developer: Kuray Ogun — solo developer, Coursion

The story

AI Stats exists because its developer kept slamming into Claude and ChatGPT limits mid-session with no warning. It puts every AI subscription's remaining capacity one glance away — in the Mac menu bar and on the iPhone Lock Screen — without routing a single byte through third-party servers.
